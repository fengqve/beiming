# A quieter reading list

Original fictional example created for Beiming; not a report of measured product results.

## Source material

A reading app replaced its long “saved for later” list with three shelves:
“I have a question”, “I am exploring”, and “I can use this now”.
Saving an item prompts the reader to add an optional one-line reason. The app shows
that reason beside the item when it resurfaces. Readers can leave it blank.
Items are never deleted automatically. The designer believes this will help people
return to reading, but has not yet measured return visits or completion rates.

## User context and request

I collect references for occasional writing projects. I already tag them by topic,
but after a month I often cannot remember why I saved a particular link.
I do not want another categorization chore or a new app.
What can I learn or borrow from this? Please analyze it; do not change my system.
